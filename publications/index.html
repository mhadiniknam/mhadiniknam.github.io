    SRIQL: Implicit Q Learning for Ultralight-weight Image Super Resolution
    Farzan Niknejad, Mohammad Hadi Niknam, and Javad Alirezaie

  6. BSc Thesis
    HAS-RL: Human-Adaptive Safe Reinforcement Learning for Clinician-Configured Force Safety in Robotic Ultrasound Spinal Scanning
    Mohammad Hadi Niknam
    Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic), 2026
    Abs Bib PDF Supp Slides Website

    BSc Thesis introducing HAS-RL, an operator-conditioned safe reinforcement learning framework for robotic ultrasound spinal scanning. Delivers zero-shot force safety under dynamic runtime limits, the Smoothed Bandit-Lagrangian (SBL) multiplier method, anticipatory trajectory tracking, and two-sided acoustic window constraints.
